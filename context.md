# Hands of Peel — Project Context

## Project Summary

This project is a hackathon prototype for **InnoHack** under the theme:

**Revitalising Traditional Craftsmanship**  
Partner: **Crafts on Peel**

The concept is a **low-maintenance digital heritage and engagement platform** for Crafts on Peel, designed to:

- preserve artisan stories
- archive past exhibitions
- promote events and workshops
- support sustainable growth through curated craft sales and donations
- reduce staff workload through easy content management

The working project name is:

# Hands of Peel

Tagline idea: **Preserving craft, extending stories**

## About Crafts on Peel

Crafts on Peel is a local non-profit charitable foundation that aims to:

- revive
- reinterpret
- perpetuate traditional craftsmanship

It does this by fostering collaborations between traditional craftsmen and contemporary artisans through apprenticeship and exchange of techniques, with the aim of nurturing younger generations to incorporate traditional skills into innovative contemporary design.

### Important identity/story details from their About page

- Located in a historic 1948 walk-up building in Central, Hong Kong
- The essence of craftsmanship lies in the hands
- Their logo is based on the Chinese character **手** (“hand”) and its mirrored form, forming **我**, symbolizing:
  - each craft begins with a personal story
  - the relationship between master and apprentice
  - intergenerational transmission of skill
- The building itself symbolizes connecting history with the future:
  - old masonry / exposed brick
  - modern materials and finishes
  - juxtaposition of new and old

This identity is important for the visual and narrative direction of the site.

## Insights from Site Visit / Staff Interview

### Current operational situation

Crafts on Peel currently has only **3 staff members**.

### What they do

- Research traditional craftsmanship topics
- Decide what crafts to exhibit next
- Recruit / find craftsmen
- Run about **2 exhibitions per year**
- Bring selected works to **London exhibition / London Craft Week once per year**
- After each exhibition, pieces are moved into an off-site storage room
- Their funding mostly comes from **corporate donations**

### Main challenges

1. **Low manpower**
2. **Limited funding**
3. Marketing / social media is demanding and distracting
4. They want to focus more on research and curatorial work
5. Their current website is poor and does not reflect their mission or branding
6. They depend on a third-party vendor for the website, and the result is unsatisfactory
7. Past exhibitions lose visibility once they end and works go into storage
8. They are not strong in branding / digital presentation

### Existing website issues

- Landing page mostly shows:
  - hero section
  - About Us
  - Crafts Fellows
- Exhibition pages mostly show:
  - poster image
  - foreword
  - exhibition summary
  - flippable PDF brochure
  - participating artisan images
- Weak storytelling and weak archive value
- Poor branding and digital user experience

## Core Problem Statement

Crafts on Peel plays an important role in preserving and reinterpreting Hong Kong’s traditional craftsmanship, but with only three staff members and limited funding, much of its work is difficult to sustain and scale digitally.

Past exhibitions are stored away after each cycle, artisan stories are not fully accessible online, and digital outreach takes valuable time away from research and curation.

---

## Final Concept Statement

**Hands of Peel is a digital heritage and engagement platform for Crafts on Peel that preserves artisan stories, archives exhibitions, and supports sustainable growth through events, workshops, donations, and curated craft sales. Designed for a small NGO team, it reduces website maintenance burden while extending the life and reach of every exhibition, making Hong Kong’s traditional craftsmanship more accessible to younger audiences, supporters, and future collaborators.**

Alternative wording:

**Hands of Peel is a low-maintenance digital heritage platform for Crafts on Peel that preserves artisan stories, extends exhibitions into a lasting online archive, and creates sustainable engagement through events, workshops, donations, and curated craft sales.**

## Community / Storytelling Layer from Teammate Discussion

A potential teammate contributed ideas that should be selectively integrated into the project.  
These ideas are not incompatible with the digital platform, but should support it rather than replace it.

### Good ideas to incorporate

#### Memory and emotion

Traditional craftsmanship is not only about passing down skills, but also memories, emotions, and everyday meaning.

#### Craft in everyday life

Create content that reconnects craft forms to things people have seen in Hong Kong life, film, streets, ritual objects, homes, or cultural memory.

Examples:

- galvanised steel in old HK shopfronts
- bamboo in household objects
- embroidery in family / ceremonial items
- gilded temple objects

This can become a homepage / exhibition section:

- **Craft in Everyday Life**
- **Where You’ve Seen This Craft**
- **Craft Memory Cards**

#### Art master storytelling

Use short videos / quote cards / “master stories” content to attract and educate visitors.

#### Collaboration pathways

Add a page / section for:

- universities
- NGOs
- schools
- community workshops
- intergenerational workshops

#### HKBU outreach

Suggest that Crafts on Peel can host workshops at HKBU via the Office of Student Affairs to:

- reach younger audiences
- generate workshop income
- increase visibility

### Ideas to keep as future / optional

- “What AI got wrong” educational social posts
- broad community wellbeing / relaxation programming
- large-scale intergenerational programming as the main product
- awards / competitions as a core feature

### Team alignment note

If working with a non-technical teammate, the best role split is:

- technical builder: architecture, coding, CRUD, deployment
- non-technical teammate: storytelling, copywriting, content strategy, workshop / collaboration concepts, pitch narrative
